What I help with

Services

I offer virtual individual therapy for adults across California, Florida, and Washington. Sessions are collaborative, practical, and tailored to your goals.

Anxiety & Stress

Build coping skills, reduce overwhelm, and create steadier day-to-day routines, including money and financial stress.

Depression

Work toward motivation, self-compassion, and meaningful connection.

Burnout & Work Stress

Set boundaries, manage pressure, and rebuild balance and clarity.

Career Transitions, Layoffs & Job Loss

Support for the emotional side of layoffs, job loss, and career change — including the identity hit when work has been the main thing, and the particular pressures of a tech career.

Relationships & Communication

Strengthen communication, attachment patterns, boundaries, and trust.

Dating & Life Transitions

Navigate big changes, identity shifts, and decision points with support.

Problem Solving & Social Confidence

Build practical tools for confidence, connection, and everyday challenges.

Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD)

Support with focus, motivation, organization, emotional regulation, and navigating work or relationships.

Autism

Support with social connection, identity, communication, and navigating the world in a way that feels authentic.
