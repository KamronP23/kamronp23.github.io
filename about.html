About Me

I am a Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist working with adults across California, Florida, and Washington, entirely through secure online sessions. Together we work through anxiety, burnout, relationship challenges, and life transitions.

My approach is collaborative, practical, and grounded in evidence-based practices. I believe clients are the experts in their own lives, and therapy works best when it is tailored, supportive, and intentional.
