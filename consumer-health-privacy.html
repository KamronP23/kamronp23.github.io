Washington Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective August 23, 2026. Last updated August 23, 2026.

This policy applies to residents of Washington State and is provided under the Washington My Health My Data Act, RCW 19.373. It covers only consumer health data as that term is defined in the Act. My general website privacy practices are described separately in my Privacy Policy, and the health information I keep about people who become clients is governed by HIPAA and described in my Notice of Privacy Practices.

Categories of consumer health data I collect, and why

CategoryPurpose and how it is used
Information you send me through the contact form or by email — your name, email address, the state you are located in, and anything you choose to write about why you are reaching out To respond to your inquiry, determine whether I am licensed in your state and able to work with you, and schedule a consultation. Nothing else.
Information that may be inferred from the pages you visit on this site — for example, visiting a page about anxiety, men's mental health, or financial stress — together with your IP address and analytics identifiers To understand which pages people find useful and to maintain and improve the site. I do not use this to build advertising profiles, and I do not use it to target advertising to you.

Categories of sources

  • Directly from you, when you submit the contact form or email or call me.
  • Automatically from your device and browser when you visit this website, through analytics technologies described below.

Categories of consumer health data that is shared

I do not sell consumer health data, and I never will. Selling consumer health data without a signed authorization is prohibited by the Act, and it is not something I would do in any case.

The categories of consumer health data that are shared, and with whom:

Category sharedCategories of third partiesWhy
Contact-form contents, including anything you write about your reason for reaching out Website form processing vendor; email service provider These vendors transmit and store the message so that it reaches me.
Website usage data and inferences from pages visited, including IP address and analytics identifiers Website analytics and tag management vendor; professional directory badge provider whose script runs on this site Site measurement and display of my professional directory verification.

Specific affiliates: I have no affiliates. This is a solo practice and no parent, subsidiary, or commonly-controlled entity receives consumer health data.

The third parties that could receive consumer health data are Formspree (form processing), Google (Analytics and Tag Manager), Psychology Today (verification badge script), and Headway (insurance billing and scheduling).

Your rights under RCW 19.373.040

If you are a Washington resident, you have the right to:

  • Confirm whether I am collecting, sharing, or selling your consumer health data, and to access that data, including a list of all third parties and affiliates with whom I have shared or sold it, along with an active contact method for each.
  • Withdraw consent to the collection and sharing of your consumer health data.
  • Delete your consumer health data.

How to exercise these rights

Email me at kamronpoosti@kindredmentalhealth.com with the subject line “Washington Consumer Health Data Request,” and tell me which right you want to exercise. I will respond within 45 days. If I need more time, I will tell you why within that first 45 days and may take up to 45 additional days. There is no charge for this.

If I deny your request

You may appeal by replying to my decision, or by emailing kamronpoosti@kindredmentalhealth.com with the subject line “Appeal.” I will respond in writing within 45 days, explaining the reasons for my decision. If I deny your appeal, I will give you a method to contact the Washington State Attorney General to submit a complaint. You may also contact that office directly at atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.
