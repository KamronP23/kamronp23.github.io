Executives & senior leaders

Therapy for Executives and Senior Leaders

Individual psychotherapy for people carrying a senior role, with a licensed therapist who works with executives. Online, for adults in California, Washington, and Florida.

Free 15-Minute Consultation

What does therapy for executives cover?

It’s one-on-one psychotherapy for senior leaders and C-suite executives, held by video. We can work on anxiety, depression, burnout and work stress, layoffs or the fear of them, navigating the AI shift, and life and career transitions. You set what we focus on, and it doesn’t have to stay inside work.

Common concerns I help with:

  • • Anxiety
  • • Depression
  • • Burnout and work stress
  • • Layoffs, or the fear of layoffs
  • • Navigating the AI shift and what it means for your work
  • • Life and career transitions
  • • ADHD, with a neuro-affirming approach

If you work in tech but not in a senior leadership role, therapy for people working in tech may be a closer fit.

Have you worked with people in roles like mine?

Yes. Executives and IT professionals are part of my caseload. Clients have included C-suite executives, among them CEOs, CMOs, and CFOs, as well as high-level engineers, product managers, and project managers. You don’t need to explain what a senior role involves before we can get to what it’s doing to you.

I also have a background in software development, and I’m currently building a mental health tech company. The technical and business side of your work is familiar ground.

What happens in sessions?

Sessions are 50 minutes, one-on-one, by video. Some of the work is practical and forward-looking: what’s in front of you this week and what you want to do about it. Some of it is reflective: noticing the patterns that keep showing up and where the pressure comes from in the first place.

Depending on what you need, I draw on cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), solution-focused, person-centered, insight-oriented, and Gestalt approaches, along with mindfulness and psychoeducation.

Executive coach or therapist?

If you’ve been looking at executive or leadership coaching, you probably want something practical and focused on your work. You don’t have to give that up to work with a therapist. Practical, solution-focused work is part of how I do therapy.

The difference is training and licensure. I’m a licensed marriage and family therapist in California and Washington, so this is psychotherapy, not coaching. When the pressure of the role is tangled up with anxiety, depression, burnout, or ADHD, that is what therapy is built to work with, and we can work on it in the same sessions as the day-to-day demands of the job.

Who do you work with, and in which states?

Adults 18 and over, in individual therapy. I’m a licensed marriage and family therapist (LMFT) in California and Washington, and registered to provide telehealth in Florida. I’ve been in the mental health field for over 10 years. All sessions are by telehealth; there is no physical office. I don’t offer couples therapy or work with minors.

What does it cost?

In Washington, therapy is private pay: $250 per 50-minute session, with a sliding scale available. Insurance is accepted in California and Florida. Full details are on the fees page.

All services are provided via secure telehealth. I work with adults located in California, Florida, and Washington.
